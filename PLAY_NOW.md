## 🎯 The 8 Games Explained

### 1. 🐦 **FLAPPY BIRD** - Endless Flight
- **Goal**: Fly through as many pipes as possible
- **How to Play**: Press SPACE to flap/jump, avoid pipes
- **Best For**: Quick reflexes and timing
- **Playability**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Easy & Fun
- **Tip**: Gentle gravity makes it more forgiving for kids

### 2. 🐍 **SNAKE** - Cyber Slither  
- **Goal**: Eat food and grow longer without hitting walls or yourself
- **How to Play**: Arrow keys to move, collect red food
- **Best For**: Strategy and planning
- **Playability**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Balanced
- **Tip**: Plan your path 3-4 moves ahead

### 3. 🏓 **PONG** - Twin Neon Paddle
- **Goal**: Bounce the ball past opponent's paddle
- **How to Play**: Arrow keys to move paddle, first to 11 wins
- **Best For**: Reflexes and anticipation
- **Playability**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Classic Arcade
- **Tip**: AI opponent is smart - aim for edges for spin

### 4. 👾 **MONSTER RUN** - Lava Runner
- **Goal**: Jump over obstacles and collect coins
- **How to Play**: SPACE to jump, collect yellow coins
- **Best For**: Timing and rhythm
- **Playability**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Super Fun!
- **Tip**: Jump early to avoid obstacles

### 5. 👽 **SPACE INVADERS** - Alien Defense
- **Goal**: Shoot down waves of aliens before they reach you
- **How to Play**: Arrow keys to move, SPACE to shoot
- **Best For**: Action and strategy
- **Playability**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Intense!
- **Tip**: Clear each wave to face new challenges

### 6. 🧩 **TETRIS** - Block Stacker
- **Goal**: Complete lines by stacking falling blocks
- **How to Play**: Arrows to move/rotate, SPACE to drop
- **Best For**: Puzzle solving and planning
- **Playability**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Classic!
- **Tip**: Keep corners and edges open

### 7. 🔢 **2048 PUZZLE** - Tile Synthesis
- **Goal**: Merge tiles to reach 2048 and beyond
- **How to Play**: Arrow keys to slide tiles together
- **Best For**: Logic and number sense
- **Playability**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Relaxing
- **Tip**: Save corners for high-value tiles

### 8. 🧱 **BREAKOUT** - Brick Breaker
- **Goal**: Break all bricks before ball goes past paddle
- **How to Play**: Arrow keys to move paddle, SPACE to launch
- **Best For**: Precision and patience
- **Playability**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Satisfying
- **Tip**: Use paddle edges to angle shots

## 💡 Pro Tips for Each Game

### 🐦 Flappy Bird
- Tap gently and frequently for control
- Don't wait for perfect gaps - act early
- Practice makes perfect!

### 🐍 Snake
- Don't trap yourself in corners
- Create spiral patterns to maximize space
- Think 3 moves ahead

### 🏓 Pong
- Aim for paddle edges for stronger hits
- Anticipate ball physics
- Move early, not late

### 👾 Monster Run
- Build rhythm with obstacle patterns
- Jump slightly before obstacles
- Enjoy the flow of movement

### 👽 Space Invaders
- Clear waves completely for bonuses
- Pace your shots strategically
- Watch for pattern changes

### 🧩 Tetris
- Keep your stack flat and low
- Save edges for quick pieces
- Clear multiple lines for bonuses

### 🔢 2048
- Plan 3+ moves ahead
- Keep largest tiles in corners
- Don't rush - this game rewards patience

### 🧱 Breakout
- Use side of paddle to angle shots
- Break center bricks first
- Catch power-ups when possible

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

**"Game won't launch"**
- Make sure you're in the right folder
- Check Python 3.8+ is installed
- Try: `python -c "import pygame; print('OK')"`

**"Arcade launcher is black"**
- Wait 2-3 seconds for it to load
- Make sure pygame is installed
- Check no other app is blocking it

**"Scores aren't saving"**
- Restart launcher
- Check folder permissions
- Delete minigames_data.json to reset

**"Leaderboards show 'No scores yet'"**
- Play a game and let it complete
- Quit properly (ESC key)
- Reopen launcher, check again

**"Game is too fast/slow"**
- This is intentional for playability
- Each game is tuned for fun
- Try different games if one feels wrong
